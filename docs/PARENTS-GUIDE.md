# Parents' guide: setting up Blini's Playroom

This guide takes about 5 minutes. You don't need an account, an app store or any technical knowledge.

**Playroom link:** https://almakamberi.github.io/blinis-playroom/

---

## 1. What it is

Blini's Playroom is a set of six calm toys for children of about 1 to 5 years: bubbles, singing blobs, finger paint, shape puzzles, a little garden and counting stars.

- There is nothing to read, nothing to win or lose, and no ads.
- Nothing about your child is collected. Everything you set up stays on your phone.
- Once it has been opened, it works without internet.

## 2. Put it on your child's phone (Android)

1. On the phone your child will use, open **Chrome** and go to
   **https://almakamberi.github.io/blinis-playroom/**
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (on some phones it says **Install app**), then **Install** or **Add**.
4. A **Blini** icon appears on your home screen. Open it from there: it runs full screen, like a normal app.
5. Open it **once while you're online**, so every toy and sound is saved for offline play.

**Samsung Internet:** tap the **☰** menu → **Add page to** → **Home screen**.

> Tip: to stop your child leaving the app, turn on Android **App pinning**. It's under *Settings → Security* (or *Security & privacy → More settings*) → *App pinning*. Open the Playroom, open recent apps, tap the Blini icon and choose *Pin*.

## 3. Open the Parent Corner

You can get there in two ways:

- **From inside the Playroom:** press and **hold the small dotted circle in the top-right corner for 3 seconds**. A ring fills up while you hold. (A quick tap does nothing, so little fingers can't open it by accident.)
- **Directly:** go to **https://almakamberi.github.io/blinis-playroom/parent/**

You'll see a grown-ups' check: **hold the button for 3 seconds**, *or* answer the sum.

> Settings are saved **on each phone separately**. Set things up on the phone your child uses, or send a setup link to it (see step 6).

## 4. Personalise it

**Home**
- Type your child's **name** and, if you like, add a **photo**. The photo (or the first letter of the name) appears as a little badge on the hello screen. The name is never shown as something your child must read, and neither the name nor the photo ever leaves your phone.

**Toys**
- Switch any toy **on or off**.
- Change the **order**: drag the **☰** handle, or use the ▲ ▼ buttons.
- Set the **level** per toy, or leave it on *Automatic* to follow the age setting.

**Look & Sound**
- **Colour theme:** Sky, Peach, Mint, Lavender, or **Night-calm** (dim and warm, good for evenings).
- **Sound:** on or off, and a **maximum volume** your child can't go above.
- **Music style:** soft marimba, soft chimes or music box. Tap *Listen* to hear each one.
- **Grows with your child:** *Baby (1–2)*, *Toddler (2–3)* or *Little kid (3–5)*. Higher levels mean more bubbles, more blobs, more shapes and counting further.
- **Counting voice:** in Counting Stars, a voice can say each number. Pick the language. It uses the voices built into your phone, so it works best in languages your phone already supports.

**Time**
- Turn on the **gentle bedtime fade** and pick the minutes (5–60). When time is up, the screen slowly dims, the sounds fade out and a sleepy Blini says goodnight. To keep playing, a grown-up holds the small moon button in the corner for 3 seconds.

## 5. Hand it over

Tap **Open Playroom**. Blini waves hello and the first toy starts on its own.

How your child moves around:
- The round bubbles in the **bottom corners** slide to the next or previous toy.
- The bubble with **four coloured dots** (top left) shows all the toys as big pictures.
- Every tap makes a soft sound and some movement. There's no wrong way to play.

## 6. Share the setup with grandparents or friends

In **Parent Corner → Who can play?**:

1. Under **Share this setup**, tap **Copy** (or let them scan the **QR code**).
2. Send the link by WhatsApp, SMS or email.
3. When they open it, their Playroom gets your toys, order, theme, sounds and time limit.

The link **does not include your child's name or photo**.

## 7. Choose who can play

Still in **Who can play?**:

- **Everyone** (default): anyone with the link can play straight away.
- **Only my family:** type a **family code** of 4 to 8 digits and tap **Save code & turn on**. Then copy the setup link again. Anyone opening *that* link on a new phone is asked for the code once; after that the phone remembers it. Your own phone stays open.

> Honest note: the family code is a light lock to keep casual visitors out. It is not bank-level security, because there are no accounts or servers yet.

## 8. Start over

**Parent Corner → About & privacy → Reset to default** puts everything back: open to everyone, all toys on, Sky theme, and no name or photo.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No sound | Check the phone isn't on silent and the media volume is up. Check *Look & Sound → Sound* is on. Sound starts after the first tap; that's a browser rule. |
| "Add to Home screen" is missing | Make sure you're in **Chrome** (not inside WhatsApp's or Facebook's built-in browser). Open the link, then use the ⋮ menu. |
| It doesn't work offline | Open it once while online and wait a few seconds so everything is saved. |
| Settings are different on another phone | Settings live on each phone. Send a setup link (step 6) to copy them over. |
| Asked for a family code | The person who shared the link set one. Ask them for it. |
| The counting voice is silent or in the wrong language | Your phone may not have a voice for that language. Try another language, or add one in Android *Settings → System → Languages → Text-to-speech*. |
| The child keeps leaving the app | Turn on **App pinning** (see step 2). |
| Something looks stuck | Close the app fully and open it again. Updates install automatically in the background. |
